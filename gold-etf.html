ETF ranking within gold allocation

Within a gold sleeve of a portfolio, exchange-traded funds that track physical gold are generally ranked higher than physical jewellery or coins for the following structural reasons:

  • Lower and transparent cost of ownership (expense ratio vs making charges)
  • High liquidity and ease of entry/exit on exchange
  • Purity and storage handled by the underlying mechanism
  • No GST on secondary-market transactions in many cases

Specific scheme names are intentionally omitted. Allocation weight to gold itself remains a personal risk-tolerance decision.
