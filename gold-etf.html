Gold ETF in plain words

What it is

A fund that holds gold and trades on the stock exchange. Its price moves with the gold price.

What it costs

A small yearly fee (expense ratio) plus the brokerage you pay to buy and sell.

What you need

A demat and trading account. Check the tracking difference: how closely it follows gold.

How much gold?

The age-based mixes on this site hold roughly 10–15% in gold, as a steadier counterweight to equity. It is not a place to put everything.

Questions

Why do rates differ by city?

Local taxes, transport costs and jeweller pricing vary, so the same gram can cost a little differently in different cities.

What's the difference between 22K and 24K?

24K is nearly pure gold, used for coins and bars. 22K is mixed with other metals to be hard enough for jewellery.
