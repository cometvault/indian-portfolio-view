Context first. Numbers second. GMP last.

How IPOs work

Step 1 Read the offer document (RHP) — business, risks, use of proceeds.
Step 2 Apply via ASBA or UPI through your bank or broker. Money is blocked, not spent blindly.
Step 3 Allotment is not guaranteed. Only use a dedicated IPO pot — see New To Finance.
GMP is unofficial and unregulated. It is sentiment, not a price or a promise.

What is GMP?

No — it is not a promise of listing gains.
