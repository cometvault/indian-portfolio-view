How an IPO works

Three steps from application to your demat account.

Step 1

Apply with UPI

Pick lots and price in your broker app and approve the mandate. Money is blocked, not spent.

Step 2

Wait for allotment

Popular issues are oversubscribed, so many applicants get nothing and the blocked money is released.

Step 3

Shares list

If allotted, shares reach your demat and start trading, opening above, at or below the issue price.

Questions

What is GMP?

The unofficial premium people pay for an IPO share before listing. It moves fast and no regulator tracks it.

Does a high GMP mean I should apply?

No. It can vanish overnight. Decide from the business, the price and whether you're comfortable holding it.

What's a lot?

The minimum bundle of shares you can bid for. Minimum amount = lot size × upper price band.

Why am I often not allotted?

When an issue is heavily oversubscribed, shares go by lottery to retail applicants, so many people get none.
