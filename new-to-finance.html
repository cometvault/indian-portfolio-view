Watch out

Common first-timer mistakes

Investing before a buffer One bad month forces you to sell at a loss.
Chasing tips and hot IPOs Hype is not a plan. Listing gains are never guaranteed.
Starting too big too fast A small SIP you can keep beats a large one you stop.
Checking prices daily Markets move; your plan should not.

Questions

Is this telling me what to buy?

No — it shows a shape, never specific funds, stocks, or schemes.

Must I finish the emergency fund first?

That is the safest order. If you are close, a small SIP alongside the buffer is reasonable.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan — a fixed amount invested automatically each month, usually into mutual funds.

Should I follow the age mix exactly?

Use it as a start, not a finish line. Adjust as your life and comfort change.
