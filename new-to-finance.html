Questions

Must I finish the emergency fund first?

It's the safest order. If you're close, a small SIP alongside is reasonable, which is why the plan splits your month.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan moves a fixed amount into a fund every month. It builds the habit and averages your buying price.

Should I follow the mix exactly?

Treat it as a starting point. Income stability, goals and comfort with falls matter more than any table.
