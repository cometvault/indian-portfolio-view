About & methodology

Portfolio View India is an educational reference for first-time investors. It shows process and shape — never products to buy.

What we show

Age-tilted Equity / Debt / Gold mixes, an emergency-fund path, optional IPO pot sizing, and market context for IPOs and gold.

Data sources

  • IPOs — public trackers such as ipowatch.in (GMP is unofficial).
  • Gold — city rates from sources such as goodreturns.in.

Refresh around 12:00 and 18:00 IST. Each block shows its timestamp.

What we leave out

No scheme names, no buy/sell calls, no guaranteed returns, no SEBI registration.
