About & methodology

Easy Nivesh is an educational reference for first-time investors. It shows process and shape — never products to buy.

What we show

Age-tilted Equity / Debt / Gold mixes, an emergency-fund path, optional IPO pot sizing, and market context for IPOs and gold.

Data sources

IPOs from public tables on ipowatch.in (GMP is unofficial). Gold rates from public city tables. Refreshed on a schedule; each block shows its timestamp.

What we leave out

No scheme names, no buy/sell calls, no guaranteed returns, no SEBI registration.

Educational only. Not advice. Not SEBI-registered.
