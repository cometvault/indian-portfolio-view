Basics worth knowing

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan moves a fixed amount into a fund each month. It builds the habit and averages your purchase price over time.

Active vs index?

Active funds try to beat a market benchmark. Index funds track one. Costs and style differ; neither is automatically better for every goal.

What is NAV?

Net Asset Value is the per-unit price of the fund on a day. You buy and sell at NAV, not a live market quote like a stock.

What does expense ratio mean?

It is the annual cost of running the fund, taken from the scheme. Lower is not always better, but high costs need a clear reason.

Questions

Do I need many funds?

Often a small set that covers equity, debt and maybe gold is enough. Overlap can hide risk.

Can funds lose money?

Yes. Equity and some hybrid categories can fall. Only put in money you can leave for the stated horizon.

Is past return a promise?

No. Past numbers describe history, not the future.

Should I chase the top performer?

Leaders rotate. A clear role in your mix matters more than last year's rank.

What if I need the money early?

Selling equity funds in a short window can lock in a loss. Match category to the date you need cash.
