Mutual Funds

Category-level roles within a diversified Indian portfolio. No scheme names — only how categories typically fit into Core SIP versus satellite sleeves.

This page describes structural roles of mutual-fund categories. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any scheme. Always evaluate expense ratios, tracking error, tax treatment and suitability with a SEBI-registered advisor.

Category roles (directional)

Large-Cap

Stability and liquidity core. Often forms the ballast of an equity-oriented SIP sleeve.

Mid-Cap

Growth engine within equity. Higher variability than large-cap; commonly a meaningful weight in long-horizon sleeves.

Small-Cap

Satellite growth allocation. Higher drawdown risk; kept smaller in most directional frameworks.

Flexi / Multi-Cap

Manager-driven allocation across market caps. Often used as a simplified core SIP building block.

Debt & Liquid

Emergency corpus and near-term goals. Primary home for capital that must stay accessible and relatively stable.

Hybrid / Multi-Asset

Blended equity–debt exposure. Frequently used when simplifying the portfolio or moderating equity risk with age.

How categories map to the calculator

The age-based calculator on the Overview page splits capital into four sleeves. Mutual-fund categories typically sit inside those sleeves as follows (category-level only):

  • Core SIP — Large-cap, Flexi-cap, Multi-cap, Hybrid
  • Direct Equity (satellite) — Mid-cap and Small-cap oriented exposure (or stock selection)
  • Debt & Emergency — Liquid, short-duration debt, PPF-style instruments
  • Gold — Gold ETFs / SGB-style exposure (see Gold & ETF page)
