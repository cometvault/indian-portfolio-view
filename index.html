Questions

Is this telling me what to buy?

No. It shows the shape of a portfolio and how to get ready to invest. It never names stocks or funds.

Where does the data come from?

IPO and gold figures are loaded from a daily update file and show their last-updated time and source. If the file is missing you'll see a clear demo-data notice instead.

How is the mix chosen?

Younger investors have more time to recover from falls, so the illustration leans more toward equity. It is a starting shape you should adjust to your comfort and goals.

Why start with a safety net?

Cash for emergencies means you never have to sell investments at a bad time.
