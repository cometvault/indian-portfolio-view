What is Portfolio View India?

An educational reference for first-time investors in India. It shows shapes and process — not products to buy.

Where does the data come from?

IPO and gold figures from named public sources, refreshed twice daily. Full list on About & Methodology.

Is this investment advice?

No. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold. We are not SEBI-registered.

How is the age mix chosen?

A simple, age-tilted Equity / Debt / Gold split used as a starting point. Your comfort and goals may differ.

How often is market data updated?

Scheduled around noon and evening IST on business days. Each block shows its own last-updated stamp.
