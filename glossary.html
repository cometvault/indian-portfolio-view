Glossary

Short definitions. No jargon without a reason.

SIP

Systematic Investment Plan — a fixed amount invested automatically each month, usually into mutual funds.

Equity

Ownership-linked investments (e.g. equity mutual funds or index funds). Higher movement, longer horizon.

Debt

Lending-linked investments or deposits. Generally steadier than equity, lower expected return.

Gold ETF

Exchange-traded fund that tracks gold prices. Easier to buy and sell than physical gold.

GMP

Grey-market premium — an unofficial price people quote before listing. Sentiment, not a promise.

Emergency fund

Cash you can reach in days (savings account, liquid fund, or short liquid FD) for job or health shocks.

ASBA / UPI IPO

Ways to apply for IPOs so money is blocked in your bank account until allotment.
